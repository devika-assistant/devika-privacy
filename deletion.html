Data deletion instructions

Last updated: 16 October 2026

For people who commented on @yourdevika43

  1. Send a direct message to @yourdevika43 with the words Data deletion request.
  2. Include your Instagram username and the relevant post or comment link so the operator can identify the local records. Where possible, send the request from the Instagram account that posted the comment.
  3. The operator will review the request, confirm the matching records, remove those records from locally retained comment files, diagnostic files where applicable, exported drafts, and operator-managed copies or backups, and respond through Instagram. If clarification is necessary, the operator will ask only for information needed to locate and verify the request.

No password, access token, identity document, or payment is needed to submit a request. This is a manual process, not an automated deletion form.

What deletion covers

A request concerns copies held by Devika Comment Assistant. It does not itself delete comments, account information, or published replies from Instagram or Meta's systems. You can delete your own original comment using Instagram's controls. The operator can separately review requests concerning replies published by the account.

For the account operator

Stop the assistant and close its terminal to end the current token session. Revoke the app's authorization through Instagram's Apps and websites settings to prevent future access. Remove local comment JSON files, diagnostic JSON files, exported draft CSV files, and any related copies or backups. Revocation alone does not delete files already downloaded.

Local files are in the DevikaAssistant folder. Review records before removing them; deleting the policy website does not delete assistant data.
