Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who operates the app

Devika Comment Assistant is operated by the owner of @yourdevika43 to manage comments on that Instagram account. It is not a public service for connecting other people's accounts.

Information the app processes

With the account owner's authorization, the app accesses the connected account's username and account ID; post IDs, post links, timestamps and reported comment counts; and available comment text, comment IDs, commenter usernames and timestamps. A username may be unavailable. It also processes locally generated reply drafts and review decisions.

The current app does not request followers' passwords, read private messages, or collect payment details. Its connection test uses an Instagram access token entered by the operator into a hidden local prompt. The app does not save that token to a file.

How information is used

The information is used to verify the connected account, diagnose authorized comment access, read comments on the account's posts, and prepare relevant replies or mark comments for review. Drafts can be generated in English, Hindi, or Hinglish using a local AI model. The current version does not automatically publish replies or like comments.

Storage and processing

The app runs on the operator's laptop. Downloaded comments and diagnostic reports are saved as local JSON files. Batch reply drafts can be exported as local CSV files. Interactive comment previews are not written to disk by the assistant itself.

AI drafting sends comment text to the operator's local Ollama service and model, rather than to a paid cloud AI API. Ollama and the computer's own settings may maintain their own logs or backups. The operator is responsible for managing these local systems.

Sharing and service providers

The app exchanges authorized requests with Meta's Instagram API. It does not upload downloaded comment files or AI drafts to this policy website. Meta processes information under its own policies. The website hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to serve these pages. These pages include no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, forms, or advertising integrations.

The app does not include a feature to sell comment data, use it for advertising, or train an AI model on it. Existing comments remain on Instagram unless separately changed or deleted there.

Retention and deletion

The access token is held in the connection test's process memory and is not intentionally persisted by the app. Local comment files, diagnostic reports, and exported drafts remain until the operator deletes them; the current version has no automatic expiry. Copies may also exist in operator-managed backups.

You may request removal of locally retained information associated with your Instagram comments by following the data deletion instructions. Deleting a local copy does not remove an original comment or published reply from Instagram.

Contact and requests

Contact the operator by direct message to @yourdevika43. For a privacy request, identify your Instagram username and the relevant post or comment link. Do not send passwords, access tokens, identification documents, or unrelated private information.

Changes

This page will be updated when the app's data practices change, including before enabling automatic publishing or introducing additional storage or external AI processing. The date above identifies the latest revision.
